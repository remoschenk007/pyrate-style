Our story

From the sea to Eppendorfer Weg.

A skipper who sewed leather, a friend with a shop, and a skull that became a brand.

  1. Before Hamburg

    A designer at sea

    Holger Hechtenberg learned to sew from his family and studied graphic design at the HdK in Berlin, paying for his studies by making leather clothing. He then spent a long time at sea as a skipper.

  2. 1984

    Pyrate-Style opens in Hamburg

    Holger and his friend Fritz Ahrens open a shop together and call it Pyrate-Style. Its skull becomes the first skull ever registered as a trademark.

  3. The years after

    Silver joins the leather

    The distinctive style and hard-wearing quality bring international customers. Holger starts creating silver jewellery and accessories alongside the leather.

  4. A new generation

    Giulia Christen

    After art school in Zürich, Giulia graduated in Fashion and Textile Design at NABA in Milan and trained in tailoring. She worked as assistant designer at Guy Laroche in Paris. During a stay at Pyrate-Style in Hamburg she found her love for leather and silver.

  5. 2019

    Pyrate-Style reopens

    After Fritz Ahrens closed the shop, Giulia Christen reopened Pyrate-Style. Co-founder Holger Hechtenberg came on board with her, carrying the original craft into the next chapter.

  6. Today

    Eppendorfer Weg 235

    The atelier is open Monday to Saturday for fittings, made-to-measure orders and the silver collection.

Questions

Good to know

Where is the Pyrate-Style atelier?

At Eppendorfer Weg 235, 20251 Hamburg, Germany. The atelier is open Monday to Friday from 11:00 to 19:00 and Saturday from 11:00 to 18:00.

Are Pyrate-Style leather jackets made to measure?

Yes. Each jacket and pair of leather trousers is designed and sewn in our studio. You choose the leather, we take your measurements and draft a personal pattern.

What is the jewellery made of?

All jewellery is made of 925 sterling silver. Each piece starts as a wax model carved by hand and is then cast and finished in our workshop.

Why does my piece look slightly different from the photo?

Every piece is made by hand, so the surface and shape of each one is unique.

Can I buy online?

Yes. Rings, bracelets, necklaces and accessories are available in our online shop. Made-to-measure leatherwear starts with a fitting in the atelier.
